# Surprise row and Combat-tab clock: mechanical follow-through (2026-09-23)

This pass built his chart's Surprise row (seconds before round 1) and the live round clock in the
Off-Hand Seconds box on both Combat tabs. Reasoning: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "The round clock's Surprise
row, and the live clock on the Combat tab". **Everything in "Already decided" is settled.**

## Already decided

- The surprise is `flags.imagine-rpg.surprise = { seconds (1-5), spent: [{ seconds, hand, label }],
  carryOver }` on the combatant, separate from the round-keyed clock; null or 0 seconds is none.
- 1d4+1, five at most; the Game Master gives it and ends it from the Mr. Initiative window.
- An action past the surprise carries into round 1 (Carry-Over reaction roll); unspent seconds are lost;
  off-hand spends in surprise cost nothing; speed seconds do not split surprise seconds.
- The sheet box is read-only and redraws from the tracker's `_onRender` (`refreshActorSheetClocks`).
