# Buying equipment in the character generator: what the pass left for a cheaper window

The shop is built: `module/shop-rules.mjs` (Foundry-free rules), `module/shop-tables.mjs` (generated by
`tools/extract/extract_shop_tables.py` from his sheet: the ADD/BUY ITEMS panel's lists and his three price
dictionaries), wired through `module/chargen-view.mjs` (@MARKER SHOP, the new Equipment step),
`module/chargen-rules.mjs` (@MARKER PURCHASES in `assembleCharacter`), `module/apps/character-generator.mjs`
(@MARKER SHOP, @MARKER SHOP ACTIONS), the `priceLevel` world setting in `module/imagine-rpg.mjs`, and the
Equipment block of `templates/apps/character-generator.hbs`. Tests: `tools/shop-test.html` (100),
`tools/chargen-test.html` (101, +12), `tools/starting-money-test.html` (60, +1). Every rules call was the
research's recommended default, taken 2026-09-23 while the user was away -- see the DECISIONS text for
this pass. Nothing below needs a rules judgement.

**Already decided, do not re-open** (unless the user overrules one, in which case it is a one-place change):
- Steps are Basics, Race, Attributes, Class, Skills, Details, **Equipment**, Review. Money and the starting
  kit live on Equipment; `startingMoneyIsDue` fires there, not on Details.
- Payment is his `payForItOpen` line for line with `Math.ceil` in place of his `+1` (his line kept as a
  comment in `payForItOpen`); if his order refuses and the purse is worth the price, lower coins are
  changed up ten for one (`changeCoinsUp`) and his order is tried again.
- The world's price level is the `priceLevel` setting, default Medium. Only a Game Master may set a line's
  own level, and only a Game Master sees his Free.
- Hemp and silk rope are sold in 50-foot bundles (`SHOP_BUNDLE_OVERRIDES`). "1O cp" reads as 10 cp and
  "3 bp" as 3 gp, both reported. The 45 out-of-order rows are reproduced, not corrected.
- Equipment and ammunition stack into one item with `system.quantity`; armour and other weapons are one
  item per copy. Ammunition is his `isProjectileWeapon` chain OR a Missile weapon with no speed.
- Creature-hide armour (60 offers) is not sold. 15 names are aliased (`SHOP_NAME_ALIASES`); 3 offers are
  left out with a reason (`catalog.left`).
- A document's own `system.cost` is its price at every level (per ONE; a bundle costs bundle x cost); a
  document his panel does not list is sold under "Other (Game Master's)".
- Not applied at creation: Bartering/Haggling (p.148), the Master's Manual size and race multipliers,
  coin weight, any refusal on encumbrance. The load is shown only.

## 1. Release it

**What to do.** Paste the CHANGELOG entry, DECISIONS entry, UPSTREAM items and PROGRESS changes this pass
returned (they were not written into those files, because other streams were editing them the same
day). Bump `system.json` to the next minor version (a new generator step and a new world setting), and run
`python tools/build_system.py` to rebuild `dist/`.

**Done looks like.** `dist/imagine-rpg/module/shop-rules.mjs` and `shop-tables.mjs` exist; the build's
self-check says syntax-check covers every module (both are already listed in `tools/syntax-check.html`);
the changelog entry names the version.

## 2. The same shop on the character sheet's Equipment tab

**What to do.** His ADD/BUY ITEMS panel lives on the Equipment tab of his sheet (HTML 17737-18672,
`add_item` sheet-worker.js:13955-14330). Give the port's Equipment tab (the wealth panel from 0.19.2 is
already there, `module/wealth-rules.mjs`) a Buy that reuses `module/shop-rules.mjs` unchanged:
`buildShopCatalog` over the Imagine compendiums, `listShopOffers` for the picker, `addPurchase`'s affordability
test and message, `payPrice` against `system.wealth`, then create the item carried (`buildPurchasedEntries`
decides stack or split) and post his "<name> bought or added N X. It was added to Carried items." to chat.
The price level is the same `priceLevel` setting; a Game Master also gets a level select with Free, and
his "Other Cost" (a typed number and a coin, `payForItOpen` directly). Most of it is a picker mode in
`module/apps/item-picker.mjs`.

Then his Sell: `remove_item` (13811-13951) pays the character with `payThemForItOpen` (78867-78905) at a
chosen level -- the reverse of `payForItOpen`, adding the coins in the price's own coin. Port it beside
`payForItOpen` in shop-rules.mjs, same style.

**Done looks like.** A character on the sheet can buy a Long Sword at Medium out of 14 gp and is left
4 gp with the sword carried; can sell it back; a player never sees Free. Tests in `tools/shop-test.html`
for `payThemForItOpen` and in a sheet-side test for the wiring.

## 3. Creature-hide armour

**What to do.** Port his creature and armour-value inputs (`creature_from`, `creature_armor_value`,
checked at 14270-14283) and `setCreatureArmorName` (78802) into `shop-rules.mjs`, and let the 60
"Giant Chitin / Giant Leather / Giant Scales" offers through `joinShopCatalog` when both are given
(today `isCreatureHide` sends them to `catalog.left`). The created piece takes his renamed name and the
armour value.

**Done looks like.** Buying "Gauntlets(Giant Scales)" with creature "Dragon" and value 12 creates
"Gauntlets(Dragon Scales:12)" with that value; without them the shop refuses with his words
("No armor value was set for ... Nothing done.").

## 4. Homebrew kept in a Game Master's own compendium

**What to do.** The generator loads only `world.imagine-equipment`, `-armor` and `-weapons`
(`CHARGEN_PACKS`, character-generator.mjs), so an item with a `cost` kept in a Game Master's own compendium
(docs/ADDING-CONTENT.md Route 1) is not offered. Also load every other Item compendium in the world (and
the world's Items directory) into the three content arrays in `#loadContent`, after the Imagine ones, so a
stock name still resolves to the stock document first. The shop's homebrew rule then offers them with no
further change. Remove the "Known gap" line from ADDING-CONTENT.md's Prices paragraph.

**Done looks like.** An equipment item "Ghost Lantern" with cost "5 gp" in a compendium called "Our
Campaign" appears under Other (Game Master's) in the generator's Equipment step.

## 5. Small tidy-ups

- **Bought equipment and kit equipment of the same name** are two items (the kit makes one per copy, the
  shop one stack). Merging a stack into kit copies of the same document at `assembleCharacter` would be
  tidier; the kit's one-per-copy rule is its own decision (DECISIONS 2026-09-20), so merge only
  stackable documents, and only the shop's into the kit's.
- **`tools/chargen-preview.html`** was given an Equipment step (a long sword and ten arrow heads bought)
  and renumbered, but it is a browser page (Handlebars from the CDN) that `node tools/run-tests.mjs` does
  not run. Open it once in a browser and check the Equipment frame renders.
- **The shop's tooltip** shows his seven prices; the Game Master's per-line level select could show the
  line's price at each level beside its label.
- **Not verified in a running Foundry V14:** the window, the Buy/remove buttons, the per-line level
  select, the chat card after creation, the new setting in Configure Settings. The template was rendered
  headlessly with Foundry's own Handlebars against the real packs for the Equipment and Review steps.
