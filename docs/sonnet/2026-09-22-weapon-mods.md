# Hand-off: poison on a victim, and the weapon mods window (2026-09-22)

What this pass left undone because it is mechanical extension of what it built. The design calls are
made and are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` ("A poison used on someone, and a weapon mods window with his whole
Customize panel"); do not re-open them. In particular, already decided:

- Bless is a **temporary** effect (a day, figures by the wielder's alignment), by the user's ruling.
  The permanent "Blessed" is his panel's choice and is one-or-the-other with a +N.
- "A customized copy" is the user's table practice and stays a button.
- The weapon's **name is never changed**; prefix, suffix and tags are shown beside it.
- Rune damage is +Nd6+N (his dictionary and tag, not his code). Poison durations are minutes.
- `getCustomizedWeapon` gives the weapon as his combat sheet carries it and **stores nothing**.
  The plus's +N to hit/damage and all weight stay where they were (getToHitModifiers, @magic,
  resolveEncumbrance). Doubling Blade is read as his "Double Blade".

> **Weapon mods.** Every weapon row has a wand button. Bless a weapon for a day (+1 to hit and
> damage for a good wielder, -1 for an evil one), or add your own temporary effect with a to-hit,
> a damage and how long it lasts; ended effects stop counting by the world clock. Below that is the
> whole of the original sheet's Customize panel: condition, quality, prefix and suffix, the magical
> plus or Blessed, base Aura and Piety Control, magical and divine abilities, runes, energy,
> physical customizations (only those the weapon can take), spells and invocations imbued, and the
> weight. Apply it to the weapon or to a customized copy carried beside it.
>
> **Customization does what it did on the original sheet.** A Chain Weapon rolls a die more and is
> two seconds slower; a Serrated edge adds one a die; a Worn blade is -2 damage and -1 to hit; a
> Master one is quicker and +1; a +3 weapon is two seconds quicker. Runes, Blessed and the magical
> abilities count in the attack, and Foe Strike, energy, Bane and the divine abilities are written
> on the attack card for the table.
>
> **Poisons can be used on someone.** Use on a poison asks who takes it -- the targeted tokens, your
> own character, or no one -- rolls each victim's Poison Resistance, and posts the outcome with when
> it takes effect, how long it lasts, and the Endurance damage over the whole of it.
