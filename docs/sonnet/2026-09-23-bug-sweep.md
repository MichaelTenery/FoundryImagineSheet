# The bug sweep: what it left for a cheaper window (2026-09-23)

Covers 0.19.3. The sweep and every call it made are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "The bug sweep, and the
Sonnet notes' leftovers (2026-09-23)". Run `node tools/run-tests.mjs` after any of these (17 suites
headless), and `tools/window-test.html` in a browser for anything that touches a template.

**Already decided, do not re-litigate:**
- Damage (attack cards and poison cards) is applied only by the card's author or the Game Master.
  Players are told to leave the rest to the Game Master. Do not reopen the old "apply and hope" path.
- Only the Game Master begins a round (`ImagineCombat.nextRound`). A clock ahead of the round is gone
  back to, not reset. Round seconds are refused before Begin Combat; surprise seconds are not.
- Windows for one document are keyed by its uuid with the dots made dashes, never by its id.
- A combatant is found by the actor itself first, by id only for an actor that is not a token
  (`findActorCombatant`, `module/combat/combat-document.mjs`).
- A doubled sourcebook is collapsed only when every comma-separated part is the same
  (`undoubleSourceValue`). Anything else is a Game Master's own text and stays.
- The Items directory's folders and items carry `flags.imagine-rpg.directory`; only those are reused
  or cleared. An unmarked tree is adopted only when everything in it is a shipped name (the XXX folder
  is adopted whatever it holds; no Game Master names a folder that).
- A touch rolls d20 + Agility missile modifier + the declared modifier; 10+ touches, a natural 1 never
  does; dice alone on contact, no fumble, no called shot.

## Needs a call from the user first

5. **Famorian natural attacks.** His `setFamorianNaturalAttacks` (sheet-worker.js:102128) builds up to
   ten attacks from the evokes held, with damage that scales by title ("{title}d10" Acid Spit,
   Digestive Fluid), counts rolled when set (antlers, horns, quills: title-d4), ranges from height,
   raking claws sized by the claws chosen, and venom by title. They cannot be static weapon
   documents. The choice: made once when the evokes are set (as his sheet shows them, a snapshot),
   or kept up with the title (an `updateActor` hook rewriting damage). Once chosen it is mechanical:
   port the switch as a pure function beside `module/famorian-rules.mjs`, build the weapons from its
   rows, grant from `physical.famorian.evokes`, and re-grant when the evokes change.

6. **A lost limb loses its natural attack.** His `getNaturalAttackLost` (120544) reads
   `racial_standard_disabilities` and `lost_limbs_list`, both of which his damage code fills when a
   limb is severed. The port records no lost limbs at all, so the function would never fire. Limb
   loss is a rules feature of its own (and the martial-arts note already leaves limb checks to the
   user); build this with it, not before.

7. **The creature sheet cannot choose a body type, add skills or remove dropped skills.** Found by the
   creature reader, out of its scope: the sheet builds `config.bodyTypes` and `gear` that no template
   uses, and a Skill item dropped on a creature is invisible there. What a creature sheet should
   manage is a design question for the Creature row on the board.
