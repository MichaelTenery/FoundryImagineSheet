# Hand-off: casting and invoking (2026-09-24)

This pass built casting and invoking: Aura Control, the pool and Piety Control are derived; spells
and invocations are cast at targets; his doSpellAction and doInvocationAction are carried across
whole; and offensive spells are attacks applied from the card. Below is what it deliberately left
undone because it extends a pattern already set. The design calls are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`
("Casting and invoking: targets, attacks, and his own arithmetic for every spell"); do not re-open
them.

Where things live:
- rules: `module/casting-rules.mjs`
- the Foundry side: `module/casting-actions.mjs`
- stand-ins for his calls that change the caster: `module/casting-helpers.mjs`
- his code, generated by `tools/extract/extract_casting.py`: `module/casting-titles.mjs` and
  `module/casting-worker.mjs`

**Never edit the two generated modules by hand.** Change the extractor and run it again.

After any change, run `node tools/run-tests.mjs casting` (79). Then run all of `node tools/run-tests.mjs`
(18 suites, 2,254). Look at `tools/magic-preview.html` served over HTTP. The Mage at the bottom and
the three cast cards are this pass's.

## 2. A multi-bolt spell's seconds

The cast card's Spend button uses the spell's cast-time column ("2 sec."). A storm of bolts takes
that time for EACH bolt: his text says "setting the casting time of 2 seconds per bolt", and each
attack is written "AURA BOLT 3(sec. 6)".

- **What to do:** in `castSpell` (casting-actions.mjs), when the cast recorded more than one attack,
  spend the largest "(sec. N)" found in `tmpresult.text`. Otherwise keep `castSeconds(castTime)`.
- **Done when:** a Storm of Bolts at 8 Aura offers "Spend 8s", and a test in casting-test.html pins it
  (make the seconds a pure helper in casting-rules.mjs so it can be tested).

## 4. Spell tuning, the mechanical half

His useSpell (sheet-worker.js:161639-161769) reads spell tuning before the effect: speed casting,
stabilizing, overloading, Aura reach and burning Aura. The helpers are pure:
- getCastSpeedReduceUnit, getReducedCastingTime, getReducedTimeFail
- getStabilizeFailReduce, getOverloadChance, getOverloadFailChance

**What to do:**
- Add those six to `TITLE_ROOTS` in `tools/extract/extract_casting.py` and regenerate.
- Port the tuning block of useSpell into `resolveSpellCast`, in his order. His lines for each outcome's
  words are in that block and in the OUTPUT section after it (161888-161950).
- Add a collapsed "Tuning" section to the Cast dialog.

Aura reach and burning Aura change the caster's Aura attribute (-1 for the day; permanent). Record
those as changes on the card, as the rest are, until the caster-changes row is built.

**Done when:** each option has a test in casting-test.html with scripted dice.
