Already decided, do not re-open: a twist rolls no d20 and its damage bypasses armour (the damage
dialog starts ticked); a failed Force Twist is the Weapon Twist of its size; a called shot halves a
brawl as it halves a weapon blow; the worn-magic extras on a hand-to-hand blow are NOT read, because
there is no customized armour to read them from; the choice is stored on the actor
(`combat.brawlingWeapon`), the window's other answers are the window's own.

Each item below is a pattern already established somewhere in the code. Do it the way the named
example does it, run `node tools/run-tests.mjs`, and tick it off here.

   bumped (the pattern of every release pass): "Brawling: his BRAWLER row as a window -- a fist, a
